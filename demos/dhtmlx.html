DHTMLX has native horizontal partner items and a polished org-chart renderer. The catch is important: a partner item cannot itself be a parent. This demo therefore shows Daniel with two partners, but all three children are attached to Daniel rather than to a specific marriage. That is the exact genealogy limitation we need to judge visually.
